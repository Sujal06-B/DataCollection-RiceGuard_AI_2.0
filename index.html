How to click the images of the rice leafs

  • Ensure the rice leaf blade is clearly in focus and fills the frame.
  • Use natural daylight without severe camera flash or dark shadows.
  • Capture both sides of diseased lesions when evident.
  • Avoid capturing hands, soil, tools, or non-rice plants in the background.
  • For best results, place the leaf on a flat, neutral background if possible, or hold it steady.
